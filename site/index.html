3,950 m Before you ask

Questions from the surface.

Do I need to be able to dive or swim?

No. The cabin stays at surface pressure the whole time, so it feels like sitting in a small, very quiet room. You wear normal clothes and no equipment.

How safe is it?

Lumen‑6 is certified to 4,000 m and never dives deeper than 3,820 m. Every dive has a pilot, a support ship with a surface crew of 14, a backup recovery vehicle, and 96 hours of oxygen on board.

Will I be cold?

The water outside reaches 2 °C, but the cabin is heated to 19 °C. We hand out merino layers anyway, because acrylic feels cool against your shoulder.

What about the bathroom?

Everyone asks. There is a discreet, private option behind a curtain. On the 12-hour dive we plan for it; on the shorter dives most guests don't need it.

Is there a minimum age?

Guests must be 8 or older for the Twilight Drift and 16 or older for Whale Fall and The Wall.
