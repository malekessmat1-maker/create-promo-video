Questions

Before you buy.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets (or use File › Import). Every formula, dropdown and chart works there.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. You only type into yellow cells: dates, amounts and a category from a dropdown. The Start Here tab walks you through it in six steps.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and the file is yours. Reuse it every year, or grab the free 2028 update.

Will it connect to my bank?

No, on purpose. You never share a bank login. Logging takes about ten seconds per payment, and doing it by hand is what makes you notice your spending.

What currency does it use?

Any. Amounts have no currency symbol, so it works for dollars, pounds, euros, dirhams, rupees and anything else.

Email us within 30 days and you'll get a full refund.
